# Attribution

The `deck-house-style` skill adapts and condenses design principles from:

- **Impeccable** — https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable — Copyright Paul Bakaus and contributors, licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Adapted: anti-pattern bans (eyebrow labels, accent stripes, identical card grids, hero-metric template), light/dark chosen from the use scene, verify-by-rendering discipline.
- **design-taste** — https://github.com/madebymustafa/design-taste — licensed under the MIT License. Adapted: the design-read step, the "never" list, genre packs (brutalist-industrial, editorial-minimal, retro-print, dark-tech, quiet-luxury, corporate-trust) and the pre-flight checklist, translated from web pages to 16:9 PowerPoint slides.

No source files from either project are copied verbatim; the rules were rewritten for slide decks. Font used for QA only: Archivo Black (SIL Open Font License), downloaded at runtime by `setup_qa_fonts.sh`, not redistributed here.
